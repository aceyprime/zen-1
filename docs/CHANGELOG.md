# ZEN-1 releases

Identities are `FM-1_6` + major, minor and patch as two digits each; the FM-1 reports the identity
(`python3 tools/fm1_install.py --info`). FuMi-1's own releases, up to 1.0.3 where ZEN-1 began, are in
[its changelog](https://github.com/cartesive/fumi-1/blob/main/docs/CHANGELOG.md).

## 1.0.1 — `FM-1_6010001`

- **Sakura:** the far bank's reflection now reaches across the whole river, behind the tree's trunk too. The trunk
  had hidden the water to its left and cut a wedge out of the reflection to its right.

## 1.0 — `FM-1_6010000`

The first public release.

- **Three scenes:** Sakura (a cherry tree over a narrow stream), Koi (a garden koi pond) and Hotaru (fireflies over a
  stream under a real moon), each in painterly or pixel-art style, with a new picture at the press of PLAY.
- **Moods:** Natural (a day or a night in half an hour) and four of each scene's own, each with its own key and
  scale; changing mood glides over about thirty seconds. Every mood remembers your tweaks across power-offs and can
  be reset to how it was made.
- **The music:** notes from what happens on screen, a breathing drone, the slow tide, rests, phrases from loops of
  unequal length, echoes, Space and Warmth; Japanese pentatonic scales chosen so every key sounds right (Yo by day,
  Min'yo from golden hour, In only for Hotaru's Deep Night); sixteen white keys, sixteen notes, playing along.
- **Nature:** CC0 recordings (brook, breeze, crickets, frogs, owl, bush warbler, a bamboo knock) with synthesized
  wind and water; showers and mist that come and go. Rain sounds as rain on water does: mostly soft ticks, a few small
  ringing plips, the drops you see land heard where they land, heavier and lighter by turns, and after a shower a
  minute of drips thinning out.
- **Sakura:** a Somei-Yoshino cherry, pale sprays of flowers lit from above (and from below at night), over a narrow
  stream; its far bank a stone wall planted a new way each picture (now and then a full promenade, mostly groups,
  sometimes a few alone): cherries near-white, pink and yamazakura, now and then a weeping cherry, willows and
  evergreens, paper lanterns; rounded spring hills behind, sometimes snow-capped; all mirrored in the water, with
  ripples, wakes, petal rafts and the sun's glitter; our bank with roots, stones, reeds, rapeseed and violets.
- **Koi** of the real varieties, patterned and shaded, gliding, hovering, following and rising to feed, coming over
  when you play; a garden pond of a new shape each picture: mossy stones, iris, ferns, a maple, lily pads, a bamboo
  shishi-odoshi and its stone basin, a stone lantern; a moonlit night.
- **Hotaru:** Genji fireflies flashing every four or five seconds, slowly falling into one shared pulse; a few at
  dusk, the rest coming out over a few minutes, the most early in the night and fewer toward dawn; far ones along the
  far bank, before woods of broadleaf crowns and a stand or two of cedars. Its moods follow the night: Dusk, Festival (early, a full moon rising), Quiet Night, Deep Night.
- **The panel:** four pages of knobs in every scene (Scene, Volume, Nature, Music; FX forward, SEL back), calm lights
  only where a button does something, every white key softly lit, a status card on HOME, saves that never interrupt
  the sound or flicker the key lights; a welcome card the first time it starts.
- **A sleep timer** (SEQ: 15 minutes to 8 hours): the picture dims over the last ten minutes, then everything fades
  out to dark and quiet until a button or key wakes it.
- **FuMi-1's koto instrument** as a mode (hold HOME), with FuMi-1 1.0.7's instruments (koto, sho, shakuhachi, flutes,
  kokyū, taiko, hyōshigi, mokugyo, harp); MIDI in plays at its velocity.
- **The installer** (Chrome or Edge, or `tools/fm1_install.py`) installs ZEN-1 and can put the stock firmware back;
  the installer page, the emulator and the site work with a keyboard and a screen reader. The host simulator and the
  browser emulator run the same code as the FM-1.
