Playing

  • It starts in a scene. PRESETS changes the scene (Sakura, Koi, Hotaru), ALGORITHM the Mood, SELECT the Activity; FX steps the four pages of knobs (Scene, Volume, Nature, Music); GLO switches painterly / pixel art; PLAY grows a new picture; a tap on HOME shows the battery, scene and mood; SAVE held 3 s goes back to the defaults; HOME held (Esc held) switches to the instrument and back. White keys play the scene's instrument and the scene answers (petals, koi food, a firefly); black keys are each scene's own (a harp, pebbles and the bamboo knock, firefly flashes and a breeze). Everything the scenes do is in the guide. The rest of this list is the instrument.
  • White keys mi fa la ti do, three times, and a top mi: the ST-50's lower row. The tonics (keys 1, 6, 11, 16) are lit.
  • Black keys the upper row (fa# sol ti-flat do# re), or ornaments when SEQ is on (vibrato, trill, damp, strong pluck).
  • SELECT 本数 (the key), PRESETS the instrument, ALGORITHM the scale (IN, YO, MIN'YO).
  • KNOB 1–4 the four values on the screen: HOME 余韻, trill rate, vibrato depth, reverb; FX the sound page; EDIT the bend page.
  • LFO vibrato, ARP trill, GLO mono / poly, SEL tap: 平均律 / 純正律, hold: the tuning page.
  • OCT− / OCT+ with a note held: bend down / up (sprung); with none: octave.

Mouse and touch

  • Drag a knob up or down, or use the scroll wheel over it.
  • Click or touch buttons and keys. Drag across the white keys for a glissando. Several fingers at once work on a touch screen.
  • Combinations with a mouse: Shift-click (or right-click) a button to hold it down, click again to let go.

Keyboard

  • Tab moves through every knob, button and key; each says what it does. Space or Enter holds a button or key down while you hold it; Shift+Enter keeps a button down until pressed again. A knob turns with the arrow keys, Page Up / Page Down faster; MASTER is a slider (Home / End too). A lit button or key says so.
  • The single-key shortcuts below work while the page or the panel has focus (not in a field), and the switch under the panel turns them off.
  • White keys 1–8: Q–I, 9–16: A–K. Black keys 1–11: 1–0, -.
  • Z / X OCT− / OCT+ (the bend with a note held), Shift LFO (vibrato), / ARP (trill), Esc HOME.
  • ↑ ↓ turn SELECT (in a scene: Activity; in the instrument: 本数).

Saving

SAVE keeps your setup in this browser, as the FM-1 keeps it in its memory. Factory reset forgets it. It is the firmware, not a recording: what you hear is ZEN-1 running, at the FM-1's 44.1 kHz. The browser emulator comes from FoMni; the idea from Groove OS.
